# 📊 EDA_Findings Summary

- NAVs of most funds showed steady growth from 2022–2025.
- SBI MF remained the largest AMC by AUM.
- SIP inflows increased consistently, reaching record highs.
- Equity funds attracted the highest investor inflows.
- Investors aged 26–45 formed the largest investor segment.
- Average SIP amounts were highest among mid-career investors.
- Investments were concentrated in a few major states.
- T30 cities contributed more investments than B30 cities.
- Mutual fund folios showed strong year-on-year growth.
- Most equity funds exhibited strong positive correlations.
- Financial Services and Technology dominated fund portfolios.
- Top 10 funds accounted for a significant share of total AUM.
- Higher returns were generally associated with higher risk.
- SIP transactions were the most common transaction type.
- Investment participation was distributed across genders.
- Tier-1/T30 cities had higher average investment amounts.
- Investor counts were concentrated in economically developed states.
- Most funds maintained reasonable expense ratios.
